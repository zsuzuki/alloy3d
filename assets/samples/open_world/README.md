# Open world asset sources

Original geometry is generated by `tools/generate_open_world.py` and `tools/open_world_assets.py`.
The standalone meadow texture avoids atlas-edge bleeding on distant ground; its built-in generation prompt is in `meadow-prompt.md`.
The two atlases below were generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-27.
They are used directly, without post-editing. Models reference shared PNG buffers so the atlas is
not embedded separately in every streamed cell. No third-party model assets are used.

## material-atlas.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game environment albedo texture atlas, exactly 4 columns by 2 rows, 2048x1024 landscape. Eight equal square material swatches packed edge-to-edge with absolutely no margins, borders, gutters, labels, objects, perspective, or text. Top row left to right: (1) natural meadow ground with fine moss, short green grass and tiny olive leaf litter, muted fresh sage green; (2) warm pale sandy gravel footpath with small scattered irregular pebbles; (3) weathered light gray mountain granite with fine mineral grain and subtle fissures; (4) brown oak bark with long irregular vertical furrows. Bottom row left to right: (5) warm cream lime plaster with fine subtle imperfections; (6) aged warm medium oak timber planks with subtle grain and narrow seams, vertical planks; (7) small overlapping terracotta red-brown roof tiles in offset horizontal rows; (8) irregular gray-beige stone masonry with mortar, horizontal courses. Each square seen straight on, flat diffuse lighting, neutral albedo without baked highlights or large shadows. Material detail suited for a cohesive picturesque rural mountain valley game, sophisticated natural colors, high detail, no comic outlines, no dramatic contrast. Each material fills its square all the way to all four edges; tiles suitable for repeated use on 3D terrain and village buildings. The top-left grass tile must read green earth from a distance, not large foliage.

## foliage-atlas.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: alpha-cutout foliage texture atlas for 3D game vegetation, 1024x1024 square, transparent background. Exact 2 columns x 2 rows of equal square regions, no labels, no border. Top-left: a lush spray of many small summer oak leaves on fine branching twigs, filling 90 percent of that square, irregular open edges and little gaps, natural medium fresh green leaves with visible veins. Top-right: a second slightly different broadleaf spray, lighter olive-green leaf undersides and branching twigs, similarly dense. Bottom-left: several overlapping conifer needle branch sprays, natural forest green needles with open irregular silhouette. Bottom-right: a spreading tuft of many slender green meadow grass blades with several golden dry seedheads, roots at bottom of its square, tips near top, individual blades clearly separate. Each region is an independent botanical cutout contained inside its quadrant with 5 percent transparent margin around it. Straight-on neutral flat diffuse lighting, no cast shadows, no background color, no pots, no scenery, no ground, no illustration outline. Photographic high detail botanical albedo, moderately bright leaf colors for sunlit rural valley trees.
